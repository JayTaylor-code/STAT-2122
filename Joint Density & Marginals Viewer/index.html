QUICK REFERENCE

How the views connect

Joint density

The surface height is \(f_{X,Y}(x,y)\). For a continuous distribution, probability is volume under the surface over a region.

Marginal of X

\(f_X(x)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy\). Integrate across all Y values to get the density of X alone.

Marginal of Y

\(f_Y(y)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx\). Integrate across all X values to get the density of Y alone.

Normal model: a bivariate normal is fitted to the sample mean and covariance. KDE model: a Gaussian kernel estimate is calculated from the observed pairs. KDE bandwidth uses a Scott-style rule and is intended for exploration, not formal inference.
